# Shy & Wild design system

This is the working contract extracted from the supplied style-sheet export. It is guidance for future edits, not additional public-site copy.

## Color

- Walnut `#1A130E`: primary page background.
- Bark `#241A11`: secondary dark surface.
- Sepia `#3A2D21`: rules, underlines, and the approved primary accent.
- Umber `#8A7A64`: captions and annotations.
- Paper `#EFE5D3`: primary type and the single inverted surface allowed per page.
- Warm body `#CBBCA3`: long-form copy on walnut.
- Bottle green from the source export is retired from the public palette.
- Amber `#C87A2E` and oxblood `#7C2B22` remain reference options only; neither is active by default.

## Type

- Newsreader carries display, headings, body copy, and italics.
- Archivo is reserved for navigation, captions, annotations, and labels at 13px or smaller with generous letter spacing.
- Large Newsreader becomes lighter as it grows. One word per headline may be italic.
- Write headline line breaks by hand and avoid widows.

## Layout

- Maximum content width: 1360px.
- Desktop outer margin: 64px.
- Twelve-column grid with 28px gutters.
- Spacing scale: 8, 16, 28, 48, 72, and 150px between major sections.
- Text often begins on the second column. A photograph may bleed past one page edge.
- No rounded corners, image borders, or shadows.

## Photography

- Apply a restrained warm cast: sepia 14%, saturation 92%, contrast 102%.
- Keep motion blur, closed eyes, imperfect framing, and the physical room around the subject.
- Caption images in small Archivo with name, age, place, and month when that information is appropriate to publish.
- The page grain is the only ambient motion in the brand.

## Voice

- First person and sentence case.
- State practical details plainly and stop.
- Name the actual object: album, folio, print, file.
- Avoid universal studio language and do not invent a positioning statement before the photographer writes one.
- Do not use: capture, magic, timeless, cherish, journey, elevated, curated, or bespoke.
