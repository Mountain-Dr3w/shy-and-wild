Privacy.

Shy & Wild Photography respects your privacy. This page explains the limited information that may be handled when you use this website.

Booking and external services

Booking links take you to Pixieset. Information you provide there is handled according to Pixieset’s privacy practices. Facebook links similarly take you to Facebook’s service.

Email

If you email Shy & Wild, your email address and message are used to respond to your inquiry and provide requested photography services.

Analytics

The website is prepared to measure general interactions such as booking-link clicks and portfolio views if analytics is activated. The site does not currently set its own advertising cookies. This notice will be updated before additional analytics or advertising tools are enabled.

Questions

For privacy questions, email contact@shyandwild.com.
